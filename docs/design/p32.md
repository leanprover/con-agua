# Lane p32: packed 32-bit elements in word arrays

**Decision** (the user, 2026-10-29, after the survey `w32.md`): 32-bit values become a library
over a small language primitive, not a third array family. The memory model is unchanged (word
arrays); a 32-bit element is half of a word.

## Step (a): the primitives

**Syntax and semantics** (`L/Syntax.lean`, `L/Semantics.lean`). Two constructors, tiered only
(every 32-bit column the checker has is tiered; an untiered array is not needed yet):

* `Expr.ld32t a t i` (`A⟨t⟩.u32[i]`): element `i` of `tierA a t`, zero-extended;
* `Stmt.st32t a t i e` (`A⟨t⟩.u32[i] := e`): the low 32 bits of `e` into element `i`.

Element `i` is half `i % 2` of word `i / 2`, the low half first. `L.ld32` aborts (`oob`) unless
word `i / 2` exists; `L.st32` likewise, and is `Mem.set32 = Mem.set64 (i / 2) (setHalf32 w i v)`:
above the semantics nothing sees anything but a word update. `half32` and `setHalf32` are written
arithmetically (`if i % 2 = 0 then w % 2^32 else w / 2^32`, …) rather than with shifts and masks,
so that `omega` reads them after a split on `i % 2` (`half32_toNat`, `setHalf32_toNat`).

**Compiler** (`Compiler/Compile.lean`).

* Load (`ld32Code x t d ln bs`, 6 instructions): `t := x; t >>= 1; cmp t, [len]; jae ca_error;
  t := [base]; movl d32, [t + 4 x]`. The bounds check compares `i >> 1` with the length in words.
  Both expression compilers have it: `compileE` (index in `rax`, scratch `rcx`) and the register
  stack `gen` (`genLd32`: the index a local's register or computed into `d`, one scratch), with the
  tier in `rsi` (the tier local) or computed (`tierDesc`).
* Store (`st32Code`, 7 instructions after the value and the index are in `rdx`, `rax`): the value
  to temporary 0, `rdx := rax >> 1`, the bounds check, `rcx := [base]`, the value back,
  `movl [rcx + 4 rax], edx`. Only the fallback statement compiler (no register-stack store): the
  stores are rare (one per new node). `tempsS` gives it at least one temporary.

Kraken covers the 32-bit moves without change: a `.W32` register write zero-extends
(`Reg64s.set`), `MachineData.load`/`store` take any width, and scale 4 is the SIB width `.W32`.

**The key lemma** (`Compiler/Mem32.lean`), on Kraken's byte memory, for the word `w` that `rdW`
reads at `X`:

* `load32_of_rdW`: the 4-byte load at `X + 4 (i % 2)` is `half32 w i`;
* `store32_eq`: the 4-byte store of `v` at `X + 4 (i % 2)` **is** (as a map) the 8-byte store at
  `X` of `setHalf32 w i (v mod 2^32)`.

Both by bytes: `rdW_get?` reads the 8 bytes off a word, `toBytes_getElem` the bytes a store
writes; the arithmetic is `omega` after splitting the byte index (no `bv_decide`). With
`store32_eq` the 32-bit store reuses the word store's memory-relation proof (`Inv.store64`,
`Pres.store64`) unchanged.

**Simulation** (`Compiler/Sim32.lean`): `ld32Code_spec` and `st32Code_spec`, over the array's
descriptor words (`ArrW`, moved here from `SimGen.lean`), used by `sim_ld32t_core`/`_tail`
(`SimExpr.lean`), `genLd32W_spec`/`gen_ld32t` (`SimGen.lean`) and `sim_st32t_core`/`_tail`/
`sim_st32t` (`SimStore.lean`), each a copy of its 64-bit sibling's case structure. Step lemmas
`ev_ld32X`, `ev_st32X`, `addr_sib4`, `low32_toInt` (`StepGen.lean`).

**Every match over `Expr`/`Stmt`** got its arm (about 40 files: `Agree`, `DefAssign(Sim)`,
`GKeep`, `ErrSpec`, `WriteSet`, `PureEval`, `FuelMono`, `WF(Fast)`, `RunEq`, `Prelude(Sim)`,
`ParOwned`, `Ser`/`Trace`, `Rom/*`, `ConsLib(Spec)`, `SlotGrow`, `FreezeSpec`, `FrontPreSimE`, …).
`scripts/perfgap/l2c_abi.py` (the L→C translator) has `l32t`/`s32t`. `scripts/leafgen/LGEmit.lean`
is left alone: it no longer elaborates against the current syntax anyway (`.abort` without code).

**Generator** (`L/ModelGen.lean`): `A⟨t⟩.u32[i]` is `L.Arena.p32Get (m.arr64 (tierA A t)) i`,
`A⟨t⟩.u32[i] := e` is `Mem.set32`; the steps get `L.ld32`/`L.st32` (`noteVcg`).

**Tests.** `Compiler/Tests.lean`: differential tests of a function that stores and loads 32-bit
elements in both tiers (computed tier, loop index, out-of-bounds in one tier only, the halves of a
word after two stores) against Kraken's semantics. `L/ModelGenTest.lean`: `tP32`, an `l_model`
with a 32-bit store and two loads, and an example on its model.

## Step (b): the library

`Arena/P32.lean`. A packed column's element is `p32Get a i` (`half32` of `rdW a (i / 2)`, `0` past
the end); its store is `Mem.set32`; its length in elements `p32Len n = 2 n`. The specification is
the equations a caller needs, never halves:

* `p32Get_set32`: after `set32 A k v`, element `k` of `A` is `v % 2^32` (in bounds), the others
  unchanged (the shape of `rdW_set64`);
* `Mem.arr64_set32_ne`, `Mem.size_set32` (frames);
* `p32Get_lt` (`< 2^32`), `p32Get_eq` (the bridge from `L.ld32`'s unfolding, a `grind` pattern);
* `ld32_p32_spec`: the load as `p32Get` for `vcgen` (as `ld64_rdW_spec`).

The accessors are L's own forms (`A⟨t⟩.u32[i]`), not calls: a call per access would cost more
than the packing saves (`der_b` is 11 % of the cycles on `Init`).

## Step (c): the derived words on packed columns

The five derived columns `ADER`, `LADER`, `FADER`, `LEDER`, `PJDER` (one word per node until now,
31 bits used) hold their derived words as packed 32-bit elements: element `i` of tier `t` is node
`i`'s word. All five moved, not only the three binary tables: the relation `cP` is one definition
for all five, and the two small ones cost nothing more.

* **Readers.** `der_b` (`x := ADER⟨t⟩.u32[i]`, …) and `fvar_push`'s level-parameter read
  (`lpBlkF`). Their models (`derBW`, `derFW`, `exprLPW`) read `pcol m ADER t i`, the library's
  element of tier `t` (`p32Get` of `m.arr64 (tierA ADER t)`), in place of the word column's
  `tfld m APP_DER t i 0`. The `TRec`s `APP_DER`, … are no longer read (kept: they still document
  the count words).
* **Writers.** `mk_app`, `mk_lam`, `mk_forall`, `mk_let`, `mk_proj`: the capacity test is
  `len(ADER⟨tr⟩) * 2 <= q` (elements, not words), the stores `ADER⟨tr⟩.u32[q] := …`. Their tail
  `derPutW` writes `Mem.set32` after the test `p32Len (len64 …) ≤ idx`; `pushZ` (the zeroed slot
  `mk_*` write before reading the children) is a `set32`.
* **Relation.** `cP A m t i _ d` is `p32Get (m.arr64 (tierA A t)) i = packBFL …`. The proofs moved
  from `rdW_set64` to `p32Get_set32`; the stored word fits 32 bits (`packBFL_mod32`, from the
  31-bit packing). New: `pcol_arr_eq`, `pcol_pushZ_other`, `pcol_pushZ_der`, `p32Get_oob`,
  `Mem.set32_set32_same`, `OutSa.set32`, `ld32_pcol_spec`.
* **The frozen image (threaded mode).** A packed column of `n` elements is `(n + 1) >> 1` words:
  `fzPCols` with the extent `fzPExt`, the other one-word columns unchanged (`fzCols`). The
  truncation theorem for `DerR` (`DerT2.trunc`, `rdpcol_trunc`) reads below the count through
  the halved extent; it needs the count below `2^63` (the tables' cap, `ConsR.cap`, gives
  `≤ 2^27`). The header keeps its entries and their order.
* **Assembler file.** The trusted parser (`AsmFile/Parse.lean`) reads `movl MEM, %R32` and
  `movl %R32, MEM`, Kraken's syntax for the two forms the compiler emits; Kraken's own parser
  agrees (`asm_file.py`'s cross-check). 18 `movl` in `checker.s`: the loads of `der_b` and of
  the level-parameter block (spliced into each function that reads it), the stores of the five
  `mk_*`.

No deviation: con-ron keeps the derived word per node in its table's row (`u64`, a hash in the
high half); L keeps the 31 used bits, two nodes per word. (The handles and stamps con-ron holds as
`u32` are packed in L's records already, `w32.md` §3.)

## Measurements (2026-10-29, master 56328b60 against p32c 8de2d41a, load 2–10)

| run | master | p32 | Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| one thread | 742.8 MB | 715.6 MB | **−27.2 MB (−3.7 %)** |
| `--jobs=16` | 1000.5 MB | 953.8 MB | **−46.7 MB (−4.7 %)** |
| parse only | 645.6 MB | 620.7 MB | −24.9 MB |

Instructions (`perf stat`, one thread): 239.70 G → 241.17 G (+0.6 %: the shift and move of each
bounds check); cycles 80.39 G → 78.98 G (−1.8 %).

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

The survey's prototype (bare `movl`, no halved bounds check, one thread only) measured −2.1 %
under load; the proved version, with two more instructions per load, gives −1.4 % on a quiet
machine. The parse phase builds few compound nodes' derived words relative to its interning, so
its change is within noise.

Verdicts: `diff-e2e --refs --parallel 16` and `diff-e2e --jobs 4`: all 634 fixtures agree, no
REFDIFF.
