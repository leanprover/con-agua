# Lane d40: clear the `replace_fvars` memo where the twin's map ends

Task: lnext3's rank 1 (docs/design/lnext3.md §1.4, "Next steps" 1), deviation D40.

## Change

`replaceFVars` (`Arena/IndPos.lean`) now ends with `if s == 0 { u := call rf_clear() }` after
the bank leave and the result check: the outermost call (the caller's generation `0`) clears the
memo (`memoClearG rfSch`: stamp `CTL[208]` + 1, count `CTL[209]` := 0). Nested calls (through
`nestHoleImg`, generation `≥ 1`) only leave their bank, as before. This is lnext3's prototype
(`_tmp/lnext3/rf/rfc.s`), now generated: the inliner puts the clear inline in
`ca_f_replace_fvars`. `rf_clear` joins `checkerProg` (1818 functions), the five lists of
`Checker/IndArmFns.lean` that contain `rf_bank_leave` (by hand), and `loopFns`, `leafFns`, `nvFns`
(their scripts). D40 moves to Fixed in docs/deviations.md.

## Proof

The memo is a cache, but the walk's relation to the twin is exact (`Bank M g mp`: the twin's map
`mp` is the slice of the memo's map `M` at generation `g`), so a clear cannot be a no-op on `M`.
It is one for the callers that matter:

* `RfInv m M G g`'s word model `rxW` gets the clear (`rxK2`: at `s = 0`, `mclearG rfSch`, `none`
  where the stamp would wrap); `rxRf_step` adds `memoClearG_spec` to its `vcgen` set.
* `GKeep M M' G g` (the map unchanged up to generation `G`, in `RxPost` and the `ds` loops) now
  holds only for a caller inside a bank: `g ≠ 0 → …`. The one consumer, `goOK_step`'s `fvar` arm
  (`GKeep.bank`, the walk's bank survives a nested `replace_fvars`), gets `g ≠ 0` from a new
  hypothesis of `GoOK` (and of `replaceFVarsGo_refines`), which `rfOK_step` discharges: the walk's
  bank is the fresh `G + 1 < 2^32`. `goStep`, `rxBin_ok`, `rxLet_ok`, `rxProj_ok`, `rxNode_arm`,
  `rxGo_nodeCase` pass it along.
* `rfOK_step` splits on `g = 0`: there the memo relates the empty map (`rfClear`, from MemoLib's
  `memoRV_clear`; the bank words `CTL[218]`, `[219]` are outside the clear's frame), `GKeep` is
  vacuous; otherwise the old proof.
* The frame lemmas (`posmemo_rx_mfr`, `RxFr` already allowed `CTL[208]`, `[209]`; the `AFr` one in
  `IndRealAbsPos`) take the extra branch.
